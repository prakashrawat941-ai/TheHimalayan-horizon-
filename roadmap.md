# Roadmap

Built in small, testable stages. Before building each stage, check it against the Master Book v2.0 (23 Oct 2026).

1. **Foundation** (this commit): app shell, English/Hindi switch, mobile-first layout, home page, placeholder pages, Supabase client scaffold, translation tests.
2. **Sign-in and roles**: email, Google and Facebook sign-in; Customer, Homestay Partner, Driver and Admin roles; role-based dashboards.
3. **Data model and security**: villages, homestays, cultural content and enquiries, with row-level security on every table.
4. **Explore**: hidden villages and homestay listings, with search and filters.
5. **Trip planner**: itineraries, estimated budgets, saved plans and enquiry submission.
6. **Cab booking**: the route rule is still to be decided (Delhi–Almora and Chandigarh–Almora only, or anywhere to anywhere). Prices and availability are never invented.
7. **Admin panel**: users, villages, homestays, cultural content, trip enquiries, cab requests, partner and driver approvals, and reports.
8. **Partner and driver onboarding**: separate flows with admin approval and partner rules.

Payments are out of scope for the first build.
