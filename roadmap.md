# Roadmap

Built in phases, following the master project prompt for The Himalayan Horizon. Each phase ends with a preview for review.

1. **Architecture and audit**: done. Plan approved on 12 Nov 2026.
2. **Frontend and design system** (in progress): Next.js migration, brand tokens, layout, home page and public routes, SEO basics. Then destination discovery pages, homestay and workation listings, the trip planner interface, and account and admin shells.
3. **Database and backend**: 26 tables as migrations with row-level security; Google and email sign-in; roles; uploads.
4. **Admin CMS**: homepage sections, destinations, homestay and workation approvals, enquiries and bookings, media, audit log.
5. **AI trip planner**: routes, day-by-day itinerary, verified homestays only, labelled budget estimates, save and share.
6. **PWA and testing**: manifest, service worker, offline page, unit and end-to-end tests, security review.
7. **Production readiness**: environment setup, production build, storage checks, deployment runbook.

Sign-in is Google and email; Facebook is not planned. Cab booking is not in scope unless approved. Payments are out of scope for the first build.
